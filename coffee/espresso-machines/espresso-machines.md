
Things to look for

- PID controller
    - Ideally easily adjustable
- 58mm Portafilter is the standard size
- Flow control
    - not sure I'd care about this but it lets you play a bit
    - not all machines have it, even high end ones
- Pump types
    - Rotary pump
        - quieter
    - Vibration pump
        - cheaper, noisier compared to rotary
- Internal materials
    - Brass
        - standard for fittings
    - Stainless steel
        - heavy but lasts long
    - Silicone
        - cheaper
        - lighter
        - won't last like stainless
    - Plastic tubing
        - cheaper
        - lighter
        - won't last like stainless
    - Aluminum
        - has to be lined with stainless steel to avoid adding taste
        - ?? harder to clean if scale builds up
- Heating element types
    - Thermo-block
        - cheapest
        - least temp stable
    - Heat Exchanger
        - mid-range
        - espresso water sent through hollow center of steam boiler
            - -- not as temperature stable as dual boiler
    - Dual boiler
        - Separate boilers for espresso and steam
- Pre-infusion feature
    - manual or automatic? (sometimes manual better)
    - see [full doc](./pre-infusion.md)
- Group head type - see [full doc](./group-head.md)
