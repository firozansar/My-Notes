## Types of joints

The types of joints you can make are

1. Butt joint
1. Lap joint
1. Dado and groove joint
1. Rabbet joint (note spelling)
1. Mortise and tenon
1. Dovetail
1. Box/finger joints
1. Biscuit, domino, dowel joints
1. Mitre joint
1. Tongue and groove joint
1. Scarf joint
1. Splice joint
    - variation of scarf joint
1. Bridle joint
1. Knapp (pin and crescent)
1. Sliding dovetail

## Butt joint

- pro: easiest to make
- con: weakest
- can be mitigated by adding
    - dowels
    - screws
    - nails
    - biscuits
    - glue blocks
- the end of one piece of wood meets the end face or edge of another piece
