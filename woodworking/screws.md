# Screws

## Square head aka Robertson head

| Robertson Size  | Color Code (on driver handle or box) | Typical Use                                  | Square Size (approx.) |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| **#0 (Green)**  | Green                                | Small screws in electronics or trim          | 1.77 mm               |
| **#1 (Orange)** | Orange                               | Small wood or cabinet screws                 | 2.31 mm               |
| **#2 (Red)**    | Red                                  | General-purpose construction and wood screws | 2.82 mm               |
| **#3 (Black)**  | Black                                | Large structural or lag screws               | 3.36 mm               |

> [!WARNING]
> Robertson and "regular" square drive are not necessarily the same.  Real
> robertson head drivers have a taper whereas "standard" square drive do not Taper
> makes it easier to use them one-handed and avoid cam-out

When the Robertson patent expired, generic square drive became more common. They
don't have taper. They use the same #0 to #4 size designations but tolerances
and fit change between brands.
